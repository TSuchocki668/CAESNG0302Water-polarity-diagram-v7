Hydrogen bonding

polarity links neighboring molecules

Solid lines: covalent bonds within molecules · Dashed teal lines: hydrogen bonds between molecules

The first panel shows a water molecule. Its bent shape places both hydrogens on the same side, so the effects of its two polar bonds add together, giving the molecule a slightly negative end and a slightly positive end. The second panel shows what that makes possible. Each dashed line is a hydrogen bond. In liquid water, these bonds continually break and re-form, linking molecules into a flexible, shifting network.
